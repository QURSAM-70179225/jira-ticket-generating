We are FitNow and we want a modern fitness app for our members.
It should be fast and user-friendly.
Members should be able to book classes and see their workout history.
We need this as a mobile app for iPhone and Android.
Trainers can create workout plans for members.
Members should be able to pay online for memberships.
The app must sync with our MBS system so member data stays up to date.
It should be secure.
Later in the year we also want a leaderboard to keep people motivated.
To keep costs low, this should be a web-only product, no app store apps.
Admins should get reports.
We want to launch soon.
