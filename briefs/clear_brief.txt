Sweet Crumbs Bakery wants an online ordering website for its single shop in Lahore.
The website is for customers who want to order cakes and pastries for pickup.
Customers must be able to browse a menu of products with a name, photo, price and description for each item.
Customers must be able to add products to a cart and change the quantity of each item.
Customers must choose a pickup date and a pickup time slot when placing an order.
Pickup time slots are every 30 minutes between 10:00 AM and 8:00 PM.
Payment is cash on pickup only; no online payment is needed.
After placing an order, the customer must receive a confirmation email with an order number.
The bakery staff need an admin page to view all orders for a selected date.
Staff must be able to mark an order as "Ready" or "Collected".
Staff must be able to add, edit and remove menu products from the admin page.
The website must be available in English only.
The menu and ordering pages are the highest priority.
The website must go live by 15 December 2026.
